Description

The Silk SubGrid33_2Grid64 command creates ...

Two ControlGrid64 blend grids meeting at a corner, before the sub-grid

The resulting SubGrid33 partial grid extending into the corner gap

Usage

  1. Select two ControlGrid64 objects meeting at a corner to generate a corner-blend partial grid.
  2. There are several ways to invoke the command.
    • Press the SubGrid33_2Grid64 button.
    • Select the Silk → SubGrid33_2Grid64 option from the menu.
  3. A SubGrid33_2Grid64 object is created.
