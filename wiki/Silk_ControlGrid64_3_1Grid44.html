Description

The Silk ControlGrid64_3_1Grid44 command creates a ControlGrid64 from a ControlGrid44 selected by one of its corner vertices.

A ControlGrid44 (select its lower left corner) → Resulting ControlGrid44

Usage

  1. Select a ControlGrid44 by one of its corner vertices in the 3DView.
  2. There are several ways to invoke the command.
    • Press the ControlGrid64_3_1Grid44 button.
    • Select the Silk → ControlGrid64_3_1Grid44 option from the menu.
  3. A ControlGrid64_3_Grid44 object, is created.

Properties

See also: Property View.

A ControlGrid64_3_Grid44 is derived from a Part feature, or more precise a Part::FeaturePython object, and inherits all its properties. It also has the following additional properties:

Data

ControlGrid64_3_1Grid44

  • DataControlGrid44 (Link): Reference Bezier Surface.
  • DataCorner (Float): Corner blending curve.
  • DataLegs (GeometryList): Control segments.
  • DataPoles (VectorList): Poles.
  • DataWeights (FloatList): Weights.
