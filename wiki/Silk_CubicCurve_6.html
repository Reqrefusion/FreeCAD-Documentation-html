Description

The Silk CubicCurve_6 command creates a CubicCurve_6 from a ControlPoly6.

A CubicCurve_6 previewing a cubic curve.

Usage

  1. Select a ControlPoly6
  2. There are several ways to invoke the command.
    • Press the CubicCurve_6 button.
    • Select the Silk → CubicCurve_6 option from the menu.
  3. A CubicCurve_6 object, is created.
