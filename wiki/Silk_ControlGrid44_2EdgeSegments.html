Description

The Silk ControlGrid44_2EdgeSegments command creates a ControlGrid44 from a CubicSurface_44 and two CubicCurve_4 segments.

A rectangular corner segment cut from a CubicSurface_44

Usage

  1. Select in the 3D View:
    1. A CubicSurface_44.
    2. A CubicCurve_4 that matches part of an edge of that surface.
    3. Another CubicCurve_4 that matches part of an adjacent edge of that surface.
      (the two partial curves cannot be on opposite edges of the surface).
  2. There are several ways to invoke the command.
    • Press the ControlGrid44_2EdgeSegments button.
    • Select the Silk → ControlGrid44_2EdgeSegments option from the menu.
  3. A ControlGrid44_2EdgeSegments object, is created.
