Create a professional 3D CAD model and exploded assembly animation of a wearable product named:

SMART BLIND AI HEADBAND

Product purpose:

A hands-free assistive wearable designed to help blind and low-vision users detect obstacles and receive directional audio/haptic feedback.

Overall design:

- Lightweight curved headband

- Modern, compact, ergonomic industrial design

- Matte black/dark gray exterior

- Gold accent details

- Adjustable comfortable inner padding

- Compact electronics distributed across the headband

- No smartphone, mandatory app, or Wi-Fi dependency in the design

Create the product as separate CAD components so that an exploded assembly animation can be made.

COMPONENTS:

1. Outer headband shell

- Curved semicircular headband

- Lightweight plastic enclosure

- Adjustable strap

- Internal cavity for electronics and wiring

2. ESP32-S3 AI processing board

- Small rectangular PCB

- Located inside the central/front electronics housing

- Include simplified PCB, chip, connectors and mounting points

3. Dual AI cameras

- Two small cameras mounted at the front

- Left camera and right camera

- Forward-facing

- Protective circular/rectangular camera housings

4. ToF / ultrasonic sensors

- Multiple distance sensors near the front/side

- Used for obstacle and distance detection

- Clearly separated from the camera modules

5. GPS + IMU module

- Small PCB/module

- Mounted toward the upper/rear portion of the headband

- Include antenna/module representation

6. Battery pack

- Compact rechargeable battery

- Located inside/rear section of the headband

- Include removable/modular battery concept

- USB-C charging interface

7. Voice/audio modules

- Left and right speaker modules

- Mounted near the ears

- Small speaker grille

8. Directional vibration motors

- Left and right vibration motors

- One motor on each side

- Clearly show left/right directional feedback

9. SOS/control buttons

- Small physical buttons

- Easily reachable

- Include SOS button and control button

10. USB-C charging port

- Mounted on side of the product

- Clearly visible in exploded view

11. Wiring and connectors

- Separate wires connecting:

  cameras

  sensors

  ESP32-S3

  GPS/IMU

  battery

  speakers

  vibration motors

  buttons

- Use realistic cable routing
